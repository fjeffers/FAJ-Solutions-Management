What we do

From the rough-in to the register.

One crew for the whole low-voltage stack, in the hours your locations are closed.

Structured cabling

CAT6 runs, faceplates and patch panels labelled to your matrix, raceway wherever cable is exposed. Rack builds, blanks fitted, and the tidy-up the last contractor skipped.

Fiber

Single and multimode runs pulled, terminated and fusion spliced. Backbone between IDFs, fiber to the demarc, tested and certified with results handed over.

POS & self-order kiosks

Registers, card readers and self-order kiosks mounted, cabled and brought up with the vendor. Kiosk tables finished properly — faceplates, biscuit boxes, no exposed whips.

POTS lines

Analog lines run, replaced and traced — alarm panels, elevator phones, fax and lift stations. The circuits everyone forgets until the inspection fails.

Cameras & signage

Interior, exterior and drive-thru cameras installed and aligned. Menu boards, Q-boards and order confirmation displays brought online and verified with the vendor.

Surveys & Starlink

Pre-install surveys with photographs and a marked BOM, so the install crew arrives with the right parts. Starlink siting, mounting, alignment and activation.
