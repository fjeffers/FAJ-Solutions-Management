What we do

From the rough-in to the register.

Structured cabling

CAT6 runs, faceplates and patch panels labelled to your matrix, raceway wherever cable is exposed. Rack builds and blanks fitted.

Fiber

Single and multimode runs pulled, terminated and fusion spliced. Backbone between IDFs, fiber to the demarc, tested and certified.

POS & self-order kiosks

Registers, card readers and self-order kiosks mounted, cabled and brought up with the vendor. Kiosk tables finished — faceplates, biscuit boxes, no exposed whips.

POTS lines

Analog lines run, replaced and traced — alarm panels, elevator phones, fax and lift stations. The circuits everyone forgets until the inspection fails.

Cameras & signage

Interior, exterior and drive-thru cameras installed and aligned. Menu boards and Q-boards brought online and verified with the vendor.

Surveys & Starlink

Surveys with photographs and a marked BOM, so the install crew arrives with the right parts. Starlink siting and activation.
